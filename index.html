Indian Market Trading

NSE/BSE cash-equity decision-support dashboard

Indian Market Trading is a Streamlit dashboard that provides decision support for Indian cash-equity (NSE/BSE) trading. It generates ML-backed Delivery and Intraday signals, shows current stock news and corporate-event context, and includes a causal backtest and a prediction-versus-actual audit ledger.

What the app does

  • Produces BUY / SELL / HOLD classifications for Indian equities
  • Records every live prediction and later compares it with the actual next-session price
  • Stores market data and prediction ledgers in a Google Drive folder owned by the operator
  • Reports backtest results net of configurable costs and slippage

What the app does not do

  • It does not place, route, or modify orders with any broker or exchange
  • It does not provide investment advice or guaranteed returns
  • It does not sell or share user data

Data sources

Official NSE public reports, public RSS news feeds, and third-party research archives used for backtesting. See the source repository for the full data-provenance notes.

Privacy Policy

Indian Market Trading · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what data Indian Market Trading ("the app", "we") collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can have it deleted. The app is operated by an individual developer and is reachable at slept-thigh-suffix@duck.com.

1. Google user data we access

The app uses Google APIs only to store and retrieve its own market-data archive and prediction ledgers:

  • Google Drive - the app reads and writes files in a single Drive folder that the operator creates and controls. File contents are market price data, model artifacts, and prediction records. The app does not read any other file in your Drive.
  • Google account identifiers - if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile identifier solely to identify your session. We do not receive your password.

We do not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Photos, or any other Google service.

2. How we use Google user data

Data is used only to operate the dashboard: to persist market data between sessions, to keep the prediction ledger, and to authenticate your session. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or to train third-party models.

3. Storage and retention

Market data and prediction ledgers are stored in the operator's Google Drive folder and in a local cache on the server that runs the app. Session identifiers are held only while your session is active. Data is retained for as long as the dashboard operates; you may request deletion at any time (section 6).

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties. Data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if required by applicable law.

5. Security

Access to Google APIs uses OAuth 2.0 credentials or a Google Cloud service account. Credentials are stored in the app's server-side secrets and are never committed to source control or exposed to the browser. Data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS.

6. Your choices: access, deletion, and revocation

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To request a copy or deletion of any data associated with you, email slept-thigh-suffix@duck.com. We respond within 30 days and delete the data within 30 days of confirming the request.

7. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
